Events & Activities

Highlights

March 2026 · University College London

Invited Talk — UCL Radar Research Group

Visited UCL and presented our recent work on human-centred radar sensing for in-home monitoring of physiology and behaviour to the UCL radar research group.

24–26 October 2024 · Xi'an, China 🏯

IEEE BioCAS 2024 — Biomedical Circuits and Systems Conference

Presented a live demonstration titled "Real-Time Gait and Respiration Analysis using Ultra-Wideband Radar," showcasing the system's real-time capabilities in gait and respiration analysis. Our team was awarded 1st Prize for Best Live Demonstration at the conference.

15–19 July 2024 · Orlando, Florida 🌴

IEEE EMBC 2024 — 46th Annual International Conference

Delivered a lecture presentation on "Capturing Gait Parameters During Asymmetric Overground Walking Using Ultra-Wideband Radars: A Preliminary Study."

24–27 July 2023 · Sydney, Australia 🦘

IEEE EMBC 2023 — 45th Annual International Conference

Presented our work on "Unobtrusive in-home gait analysis using ultra-wideband radar technology based on joint range-Doppler-time representation."

2–4 November 2022

UK DRI Connectome 2022 — Brighton

Presented our work on remote gait analysis with radar technology for dementia patients at the UK Dementia Research Institute annual conference. An opportunity to give a flash talk and present a poster on our project.

May 2022

UK DRI In Care Research & Technology Centre Launch

Presented a live demonstration of radar-based gait analysis at the UK Dementia Research Institute In Care Research & Technology Centre launch event.
